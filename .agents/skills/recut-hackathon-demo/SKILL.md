# Goal
Help the ReCut OS submission demonstrate technical competence, sustainability relevance, practical feasibility, and presentation clarity.

## Procedure
1. Verify current organizer requirements, deadlines, rubric, and demo duration at the official Devpost portal; do not depend solely on prior notes.
2. Run the publicly accessible `/demo` from an incognito browser and confirm its sample case uses genuine planner output.
3. Select one **representative, version-controlled fixture** showing remnant inventory and a full job. Record baseline and optimizer metrics directly from actual runs.
4. Open with the workshop pain point, not a list of frameworks. Show source inventory, entered job, live optimization, physical cut tree, and transparent purchase comparison.
5. Write a timed script under the official video duration; reserve time for the actual outcome and honest caveats.
6. Show the engineering differentiator: two-dimensional feasible guillotine split tree, real kerf subtraction, source tracking, and independent validation.
7. Clearly separate hypothetical/example costs, computed model outcomes, actual pilot evidence, and future claims.
8. Include a public repo, setup instructions, deployed URL, evaluation methodology, limitations, and a short architecture/algorithm diagram in README.
9. Record one concise failure case if useful: part too large or conflicting stock reservation handled safely.
10. Verify final submission links after upload. Obtain necessary participation/guardian consent required by the organizer/account platforms.

## Do not
Invent judge reactions, customer pilots, physical waste reduction, or a guaranteed winning percentage. Avoid filler buzzwords and long static slides that hide the functioning optimizer.
